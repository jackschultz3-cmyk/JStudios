The Definitive Dragon Ball Z × Dragon Ball Super × Dragon Soul Open-World Roblox RPG

## 1. YOUR ROLE AND MISSION
You are now acting as an entire professional Roblox development studio.
Your team consists of:

* Creative Director and Dragon Ball Art Director
* Lead Roblox Luau Engineer
* Senior Gameplay Systems Architect
* Open-World Environment Director
* Professional 3D Character Artist
* Anime Animation Director
* Advanced Combat Designer
* Cinematic VFX Technical Artist
* NPC AI and Behavior Engineer
* RPG Progression Designer
* UI/UX Director
* Worldbuilding and Quest Designer
* Performance and Networking Engineer
* Quality Assurance Lead

Your mission is to transform my existing Roblox project into the most complete, immersive, visually impressive Dragon Ball-inspired open-world RPG realistically achievable within Roblox Studio.
This is not a new project. Inspect and preserve my existing work.
The game should combine the best qualities of:
Dragon Ball Z: Iconic character designs, martial arts, transformations, explosive battles, alien invasions, and legendary locations.
Dragon Ball Super: Modern anime presentation, divine transformations, Ultra Instinct, gods, multiversal content, and spectacular combat.
Original Dragon Ball: Exploration, whimsical architecture, rural villages, martial arts tournaments, dinosaurs, and adventurous worldbuilding.
Dragon Ball GT: Space exploration, alien planets, Super Saiyan 4, and unusual environments.
Dragon Ball DAIMA: Fantasy worlds, expressive designs, magical environments, and distinctive architecture.
Dragon Soul (Roblox): Open-world exploration, stat progression, NPC quests, combat, grinding, transformations, and accessible RPG mechanics.
Dragon Ball Z: Kakarot: Expansive environments, beautiful landscapes, recognizable locations, character interactions, and immersive exploration.
Dragon Ball Sparking! ZERO: Cinematic combat, transformations, high-speed aerial battles, and powerful energy attacks.
Dragon Ball FighterZ: Strong animation posing, stylized character presentation, and dramatic attack sequences.
The result must be a cohesive game rather than a collection of disconnected mechanics.

### NON-NEGOTIABLE OBJECTIVE
I want a game that genuinely looks, moves, and feels like Dragon Ball Z and Dragon Ball Super.
Do not produce a basic Roblox simulator with Dragon Ball-themed effects.
Do not create a tiny map with a handful of enemies.
Do not make every NPC a block mannequin.
Do not create hundreds of attacks that are merely recolored versions of the same projectile.
Do not claim to have created assets or tested systems that do not actually exist.
Prioritize genuine implementation, visual quality, content diversity, polished movement, and a massive believable world.

## 2. UNIFIED ART DIRECTION: DRAGON BALL Z AND SUPER
The dominant visual style must be based on Dragon Ball Z and Dragon Ball Super.
Use original Dragon Ball, GT, DAIMA, Kakarot, and Dragon Soul to enrich the overall aesthetic.

### Character Art Direction
Characters must feature:

* Authentic anime-inspired body proportions
* Broad shoulders and athletic physiques

* Distinct eyebrows

Use smooth, stylized 3D geometry.
Avoid default block-shaped Roblox avatars as the final character presentation.
Create or integrate custom R15-compatible character models, skinned meshes, and animation rigs where practical.

### Environmental Art Direction
The world must use:

* Rounded Toriyama-inspired architecture
* Colorful futuristic buildings
* Large, sculpted mountains
* Detailed vegetation
* Beautiful stylized skies
* Anime-style materials
* Vibrant but controlled colors
* Carefully designed roads
* Realistic-feeling environmental scale
* Detailed interior and exterior environments

The world should be visually recognizable as Dragon Ball even when no characters are present.

### Rendering
Use Roblox-supported lighting, materials, textures, meshes, highlights, and post-processing to approximate a modern cel-shaded anime aesthetic.
Do not assume unsupported custom shader capabilities.

## 3. MASSIVE OPEN-WORLD EARTH
This is one of the most important requirements.
Earth must be enormous, interconnected, geographically diverse, and filled with meaningful content.
Do not build the world as several small floating islands placed next to one another.
Create a proper world containing large landmasses, oceans, mountains, cities, countryside, villages, islands, forests, deserts, and hidden locations.
The world should feel like an actual planet rather than a combat arena.

### World Scale
Design Earth around a large streaming-enabled playable world.
Use a target of approximately 30,000–50,000 Roblox studs across its principal explorable extent, subject to technical testing and performance.
This is a design target, not a requirement to generate empty terrain.
Use geographic regions with believable travel distances.
Players should need to fly or use transportation to reach distant areas.
Major cities should be separated by substantial countryside, forests, mountains, or water.
The player should not see every major region from one location.
